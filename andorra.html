Introduction

This is a journal of my ANDORRA HOLIDAY ⛱️! I went with my mum and with Oak Hall, a Christian organisation! Andorra is a little country sandwiched between Spain and France, and it has a native language called Catalan. It is a very mountainous ⛰️ region will hot 🥵 and cold 🥶 temperatures. The rocks 🪨there make hikes and walks difficult💩.

🈷️ They speak Catalan, Spanish, French, and Portuguese in Andorra. I already knew some Portuguese from the Portugal trip a year earlier. Although, `eu só falo un pouco de Pôrtugues!' (I only know a little bit of Portuguese!). Apparently half the population of Andorra speak Portuguese, so I took comfort in that number 🧮, I had a 50/50 chance 🎲 of either being understood or having a bad time communicating ❌🗣️.

Packing

It was forecasted to go up to 36°C 🌡️🥵! I brought 3 pairs of shorts 🩳, 4 T-Shirts 👕, 1 pair of trousers 👖, 5 pairs of socks 🧦, and much more clothing! It was quite fun 😊 to pack 💼, as it was kinda like Tetris! 🧳I also brought 2 notepads 📒, some pens 🖋️, my QiYi M Pro 3x3 🧩, my MoYu Timer ⌛, my phone 📱, and other fun games 🎮 I could enjoy 😊. Toothpaste, a toothbrush 🪥 and other essentials were brought as well. Because of the rocky 🪨 walks🧗🏻, I brought my walking boots 👢 with me!

I was carrying my Among Us bag 🛍️ 🤨 with my stuff and my mum was carrying her bag 🎒 and a large suitcase 🦣🛅. The suitcase was checked in ✔️ and my bag 💰 was a bit heavy 🪨!

Journal

29th July
🚐 to train station, 🚆 to London, Euston, 🚇 to London, Victoria, 🚆 to London, Gatwick, 👣to 🏨. In the hotel, we had a small and a big bed. At 🌃, it was really 🥵. Only 7 hours of 💤! The bus and train 🚃 🚂 were long, making it hard! 💨
30th July
Got up and treated myself to a nice Premier Inn continental breakfast . The Premier Inn was also right next to Gatwick Manor. I had a few Pain Au Chocolat, half a sourdough crumpet, and some Coco Pops. Munching on Sweet and Spicy Giant Wotsits️, I met up with Oak Hall in the South Terminal of Gatwick Airport. The ride on the plane was nice, I had a sandwich for lunch and I played a bit of Hangman. The flight was to Barcelona, Spain, followed by a coach to Andorra. The queue was really long, over 30 minutes! ⌚⏳It was really frustrating 😡.The coach ride was nice, but I was bewildered by the spice of Takis️! Dinner was already in our rooms when I arrived! I settled in nice, and the room was quite good! The entrance led to a corridor to the main room, with the bathroom branching off. The main room had two beds, with a few lamps and drawers. Past the main room, there was a BALCONY with an Amazing View!
31st July
Got up in my new hotel room, and I forgot I was on holiday! I looked past the balcony, marvelling at the mountains. I walked around the outdoor space, and saw that there was a bridge over a massive river with a waterfall, and with a swimming pool on the other side! I had breakfast and met someone who had studied language in University, and had a nice chat about Latin and Greek. I went on a 8.5 kilometre walk to Andorra La Vella, where I ate McDonald's McFlurries! To get there, I had to slide under a fence, which was very adventurous! I was quite worn out.
1st August
I had a nice breakfast and went canoeing! I canoed at Olympic Park in Spain then headed to another town, where I got an oreo ice cream. Other people went kayaking and paddleboarding. I had a big dinner, with some delicious chocolate mousse.
2nd August
I kicked the morning off with some calm mini golf, and nearly got a hole in one on the hill hole. I then chilled at the pool for a while. I was surprised that the hotel had so many activities!
